BREACHLINE: MERIDIAN — TACTICAL FPS

Original round-based first-person tactical shooter for the make.fun Three.js Tactical FPS 02 competition.

PLAY
  Use Node.js 22.18 or newer.
  Run: node serve.ts
  Open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome with WebGL 2 enabled.

CONTROLS
  WASD move · Mouse aim · Left click fire · R reload
  Ctrl crouch · Space jump · G fragmentation grenade · B open armory
  Buy-phase armory: rifle, SMG, shotgun, armor and grenade.
  When the charge is planted, move within range and press E to defuse.

GAMEPLAY
  Sentinels defend against Raiders in the original Meridian industrial yard.
  Opposing operators patrol routes, seek targets, engage, and contest the charge sites.
  Win rounds by eliminating the opposing team, defusing the charge, or protecting the sites until time expires. Round wins award credits for the next armory phase.

SUBMISSION
  main.ts copies game/ and screenshots/ into MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (or /output) and writes dossier.json using the supplied make.fun dossier library.
  The three JPEG screenshots are 1280 x 720 browser captures of this game's own rendered scene.

This source package does not include or fabricate the separate agent-session transcript bundle or a GitHub repository/commit/payout-wallet signature required by the competition's final submission workflow.
